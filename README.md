# **My Favorite Series: *Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood***

> *“Humankind cannot gain anything without first giving something in return.”*

---

## **Overview**

**Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood (FMAB)** is a Japanese anime series adapted from Hiromu Arakawa’s manga. It follows two brothers on a journey of redemption, science, and moral struggle.

---

## **Why I Love This Series**

* **Deep and mature storytelling**
* **Complex characters with real growth**
* **Great world-building**
* **Emotional moments that actually land**
* **One of the best endings in anime history** ✅

---

## **Main Characters**

1. **Edward Elric** – The Fullmetal Alchemist
2. **Alphonse Elric** – His soul bound inside a suit of armor
3. **Roy Mustang** – Flame Alchemist, future leader
4. **Winry Rockbell** – Mechanic and family friend

---

## **Key Themes**

* **Equivalent Exchange**
* **Sacrifice vs. ambition**
* **The cost of power**
* **Brotherhood and loyalty**
